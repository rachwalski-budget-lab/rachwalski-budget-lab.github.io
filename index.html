I'm a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at the Tobin Center for Economic Policy's Budget Lab at Yale University, where I develop and maintain microsimulation models estimating the revenue, distributional, and macroeconomic effects of federal tax and transfer policy proposals for congressional and public use.

I graduated from the University of Georgia's Morehead Honors College with an A.B. in Economics and Political Science (Phi Beta Kappa, Magna Cum Laude), along with a Certificate in Applied Politics and minors in English and International Affairs. My work sits at the intersection of applied economics, public policy, and data science, and spans research assistantships, government internships, and policy fellowships — from studying AI's effects on litigation and labor markets to analyzing foreign disinformation campaigns and drafting state legislation.

Research Fields & Interests
Primary: Public Economics • Tax & Transfer Policy Microsimulation • Applied Microeconomics
Secondary: Political Economy • Organizational Crisis Management • Applied Econometrics
