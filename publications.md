<p class="lede">
My published research sits mostly at the intersection of AI and institutions &mdash;
how organizations recognize, absorb, and mismanage the risks that generative AI
introduces. It is the empirical background to my current interest in what AI does
to labor markets and to the agencies that regulate them.
</p>
